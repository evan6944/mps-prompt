1Case notes

How to write notes so names are hidden
  • Define everything to hide with "role = name", one per line, anywhere in the notes:
    resident = mr ahmad bin ali, SC, 45, S7712345A
    wife = ms shirley / shirl, spr, 26
    boss = mr ah tiong   agent = guyukorkor, m
    son's school = west grove secondary   workplace = cck best ktv
  • Use "=" only for this. For other notes write "work: singer" or "letters - hdb, sso".
  • The name is everything before the first comma. Details after it (SC/PR, age, job) stay visible to the AI.
  • Other spellings or short forms go before the comma, after "/": wife = ms shirley / shirl.
  • Title gives he/she (mr / ms - never mdm). No title? Add "m" or "f": agent = guyukorkor, m. The resident always needs a title.
  • Schools, workplaces, clinics, condos and addresses get their own line, not a detail on someone else's line.
  • Numbers are hidden automatically: NRIC/FIN, phone, email, DOB, postal code, unit, UEN, car plate, long numbers.
  • Names that are everyday words (May, Hope, Mark…): write them with a title or "@" in the notes: ms may or @may.
  • Countries and government agencies (HDB, ICA, SSO…) are never hidden.
  • Follow-up cases: tick "Follow-up" or write "follow up" in the notes. Split the notes into previously:, agency reply:, changes since last letter: and current letter:. For a saved case, open it and click "Start a follow-up letter from this case".
